Privacy Policy

App Name: MRX Zombie Shooter
Package Name: com.sonulal.mrxzombieshooter
Developer: MRX Games (Sonu Lal)
Effective Date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MRX Zombie Shooter handles user information. Your privacy is important to us, and this game is designed to operate completely offline without collecting personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. MRX Zombie Shooter does not require account registration, login credentials, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or payment information.

2. Local Data Storage

The game stores gameplay progress and preferences (such as unlocked missions, in-game coins, purchased weapons, graphics quality, and audio volume settings) strictly on your device using local Android storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is permanently deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

3. Device Permissions

MRX Zombie Shooter requests only the minimum permission required for gameplay:

  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback during weapon firing and combat impacts.

The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, storage files, SMS, call logs, or GPS location, and does not require internet access to play.

4. Third-Party Services, Ads, and Analytics

MRX Zombie Shooter does not integrate third-party advertising networks, behavioral tracking SDKs, or cloud analytics services.

5. Children's Privacy

MRX Zombie Shooter is a zombie survival-horror action game intended for teen and mature audiences and is not directed toward children under the age of 13. Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Data Security

Since all game progress remains locally on your device and no personal data is transmitted over the internet, your gameplay preferences remain entirely under your control on your device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if future updates introduce new features. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
